## Privacy Policy

This Chrome extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or user information.

### Data Collection
No data of any kind is collected from users. This includes, but is not limited to, personal information, browsing activity, authentication data, or device information.

### Use of Permissions
The extension interacts solely with Roblox Inventory, Thumbnail and Trade APIs. These interactions are limited to providing the extension’s intended functionality and do not involve collecting or storing user data.

### Third-Party Services
No third-party analytics, tracking tools, or external data processing services are used.

### Data Storage
Locally stored data is limited to extension settings (e.g., user preferences) that are necessary for the extension’s functionality. No data is stored remotely.

### Security
Since no user data is collected or stored, there is no risk of data exposure through this extension.

### Changes to This Policy
This privacy policy may be updated if the extension’s functionality changes. Any updates will be reflected in a revised effective date.
